How to use

Shampoo, then treatment.

Use the two together each time you wash your hair. The whole routine takes a few minutes in the shower.

Step 1

Shampoo

Take an appropriate amount and apply it to your scalp and hair. Gently massage, then rinse thoroughly with clean water.

Step 2

Treatment

After shampooing, apply an appropriate amount to your hair and gently massage.

Step 3

Rinse

Leave the treatment on for 2–3 minutes, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.
